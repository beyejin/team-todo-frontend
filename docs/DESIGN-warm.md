## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines high-performance knowledge work through a calm, tactile, and grounding aesthetic. Replacing the stark, clinical coolness of traditional software tools, it draws inspiration from natural materials, analog desk ephemera, woven linen, warm terracotta, and honeyed sunlight.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Humane & Calming:** Dissipates urgency addiction and visual fatigue. Generates a quiet, restorative space for deep thought.
- **Warm Craftsmanship:** Feels grounded like fine stationary, natural sand, and terracotta earthenware, while preserving technical rigor.
- **Intentional Velocity:** Prioritizes unhurried clarity without compromising ergonomics, density, or keyboard-driven workflows.

### Design Movement
A blend of **Modern Warm Minimalism** and **Tactile Organic Warmth**. The interface uses soft warm surfaces, delicate amber and honey highlights, earthbound terracotta accents, and organic rounded geometry to construct an environment that feels welcoming and durable.

## Colors

The color palette grounds daily productivity in an earthy, warm-spectrum environment. Cool blues, purples, and slates are replaced with organic amber, rich terracotta, and soothing linen-sand foundations.

### Chromatic Roles
- **Primary (`#B45309` Amber Oak):** The focal anchor for primary buttons, active navigation states, key toggles, and critical milestones.
- **Secondary (`#C25E3E` Terracotta):** Warm, energetic contrast used for contextual callouts, progress tracks, urgent tags, and secondary action highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` Honey Gold):** Luminous warmth for notification pings, rating stars, active search highlights, and focus borders.
- **Neutral (`#78716C` Warm Stone):** A balanced, warm gray baseline that provides gentle contrast for non-critical elements.

### Neutrals & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF7F2` Linen Base):** High-comfort, low-strain backdrop that simulates textured warm paper.
- **Elevated Canvas (`#FFFFFF` Pure Parchment):** Used for cards, floating menus, and popovers to provide luminous separation.
- **Subtle Recessed Surfaces (`#F4EFEA` Sand Surface):** Sidebar backgrounds, input fields, and tab rails.
- **Dividers & Outlines (`#E7DFD5` Parchment Border):** Soft, non-aggressive hairline boundaries.
- **Text & Contrast:** Primary text rests in `#292524` (Deep Warm Ink) for soft readability; secondary text in `#57534E`.

## Typography

The typographic system balances the architectural, warm geometry of **Epilogue** for display headings with the soft, humanist clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body copy and dense interface metrics.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display Headlines:** Handled by **Epilogue**, lending a composed, editorial rhythm reminiscent of architectural prints and independent publications.
- **Interface Copy & Data:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** brings rounded terminals and open apertures that prevent eye fatigue during long reading and writing sessions.
- **Tabular Data:** Use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` for timers, financial counters, project estimates, and priority indicators to ensure vertical alignment.
- **Labels & Micro-Badges:** Render metadata categories in `label-xs` with uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.03em`) for immediate scannability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy implements a fluid, rhythmic structure inspired by traditional book layouts, allowing knowledge workers to organize complex tasks comfortably.

### Layout System
- **Application Workspace Shell:**
  - **Navigation Panel:** 256px wide collapsible rail resting on `#F4EFEA`.
  - **Auxiliary Drawer:** 300px collapsible panel for contextual threads, document outlines, or filters.
  - **Main Canvas:** Centered, fluid work area with bounded max-width constraints (up to 1440px for multi-column boards, 720px for focused editor views).
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1200px):** Full multi-pane arrangement; 32px canvas margins; 24px column gutters.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** Side navigation condenses to a slim 64px icon rail; work panes shift to touch-scrollable tabs.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Navigation tucks into a bottom sheet; work area adapts to a single-column flow with 16px edge padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through tactile, warm ambient shading and tonal surface tiers rather than harsh, cold drop shadows.

### Surface Elevation Layers
- **Ground Tier (Canvas):** `#FAF7F2` flat plane.
- **Resting Tier (Cards & Inset Panels):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a subtle `1px solid #E7DFD5` border with an ambient warm shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(41, 37, 36, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(41, 37, 36, 0.02)`.
- **Hover & Active Tier (Cards in Focus):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 8px -1px rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(41, 37, 36, 0.04)` paired with an `#D6C7B7` border transition.
- **Floating Overlays (Trays & Toolbars):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(41, 37, 36, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(41, 37, 36, 0.03)` and a `1px solid #E7DFD5` outline.
- **Modal Dialogs:** `box-shadow: 0 24px 38px -6px rgba(41, 37, 36, 0.12)` layered above a translucent backdrop tinted with `rgba(41, 37, 36, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic, comfortable curvature to complement its warm tones. A balanced roundedness setting (level 2) imparts warmth and craft without reducing operational space or clarity.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Small Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Badges, Tabs):** 0.5rem (8px) for an approachable, tactile feel.
- **Medium Panels (Cards, List Groups, Dropdowns):** 1rem (16px) to soften the perimeter of complex data blocks.
- **Large Overlays (Modals, Sheet Drawers):** 1.5rem (24px) for organic sheet edges.
- **Tokens & Indicators (Avatars, Status Pills):** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid warm amber (`#B45309`), white text, 8px border radius. Hover state transitions to `#92400E`. Focus ring: `0 0 0 2px #FAF7F2, 0 0 0 4px #D97706`.
- **Secondary Action:** Grounded sand background (`#F4EFEA`), `#292524` text, bordered by `1px solid #E7DFD5`. Hover: `#EBE4DC`.
- **Terracotta Accent Button:** `#C25E3E` fill with white text, utilized for creative triggers, milestones, and high-energy secondary actions.
- **Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `#57534E` text. Hover: `#F4EFEA`.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Height & Spacing:** 40px standard height with 12px horizontal inset.
- **Styling:** White `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E7DFD5` border, `#292524` text, placeholder in `#78716C`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#B45309` accompanied by a soft honey ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.18)`.

### Cards & Task Blocks
- **Construction:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed within `1px solid #E7DFD5` and rounded-lg (16px) corners.
- **Inner Rhythm:** 16px to 20px padding. Top bar highlights category chips or project tags; center showcases the task title (`headline-sm`); footer arranges assignees and warm progress meters.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Geometry:** 22px height, fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), 8px horizontal padding.
- **Tonal Semantics:**
  - *Active / Focus:* Honey tint (`#FEF3C7`), `#92400E` text, `1px solid #FDE68A`.
  - *In Progress / Creative:* Terracotta wash (`#FBE9E2`), `#9C4124` text, `1px solid #F3C5B5`.
  - *Completed / Stable:* Sage green tint (`#F2F7F4`), `#2D593E` text, `1px solid #C8DEC5`.
  - *Blocked / Needs Review:* Clay red tint (`#FEF2F2`), `#991B1B` text, `1px solid #FECACA`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Unchecked:** 18px size, `#FFFFFF` interior with `1.5px solid #D6C7B7` border, rounded at 5px.
- **Checked:** Filled with `#B45309`, displaying a white crisp check icon. Focus ring: `0 0 0 2px #FAF7F2, 0 0 0 4px #D97706`.

### Lists & Activity Rows
- **Layout:** Subtle borders separating rows (`1px solid #F4EFEA`).
- **Interaction:** Row hover transitions softly to `#F4EFEA` (Sand Surface). Active or selected rows adopt a light amber tint (`#FEF9F0`) with a 3px vertical `#B45309` left accent rail.
